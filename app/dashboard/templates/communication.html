Section 4.6 Governance & RBAC Summary

• Native Template & Signature Management: Templates and signatures are maintained within Nookal's official template editor under Settings > Practice Admin > Communication Templates.

• Role-Based Access: Staff and Practitioners have read-only visibility into communication delivery and readiness. Only Administrators and Practice Owners have permissions to modify workflow bindings or override delivery settings.

• Existing Patient-Facing Number: Nookal PMS SMS sender ID is linked to the clinic's approved mobile/landline number, ensuring seamless patient recognition without new unfamiliar sender numbers.
